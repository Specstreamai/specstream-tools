# Privacy

This plugin sends the text of each request you ask for (for example "roofing companies in Columbus, Ohio"), the number of results and your maximum price to SpecStream at `https://api.specstream.ai`, authenticated with your SpecStream API key. It reads back the list's status and rows.

The plugin itself stores nothing and logs nothing. It does not send data to any other service.

SpecStream processes requests and keeps your lists in your account as described in its privacy policy: https://specstream.ai/privacy. Questions: support@specstream.ai.
